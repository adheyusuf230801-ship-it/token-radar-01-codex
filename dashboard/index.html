02 / THE LENS

Capital, momentum,
and time in the field.

TVL anchors the estimate. Seven-day growth adjusts momentum; tracking age adds a bounded maturity premium. A small chain breadth factor recognizes distribution. No names receive special treatment.

TVL × 0.32 × momentum × maturity × breadth
Momentum
1 + clamp(7d %, −60, 120) / 200
Maturity
0.70 + 0.30 × √(min(age, 1460) / 1460)
Breadth
1 + min(6, max(0, chains − 1)) / 100

Rounded to whole dollars. Missing growth uses 0%; unknown age uses 365 days. Age is measured against the sealed snapshot, a tracking proxy rather than proof of token history. Category has no valuation premium. This estimates a whole conditional token value, never a personal allocation or a probability of launch.
